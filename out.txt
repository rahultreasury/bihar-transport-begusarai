10. NET VEHICLE VENDOR PAYABLE
This is extremely important.
The calculation is:
Gross Vehicle Hire − Commission = Net Payable to Vehicle Vendor
Example:
Gross Vehicle Hire       ₹42,000
Less: Commission          ₹2,000
────────────────────────────────
Net Vendor Payable       ₹40,000
The system must store all three separately.
Do not replace ₹42,000 with ₹40,000.
This allows your reports to show how much commission Bihar Transport earned.

C. VERY IMPORTANT — LEDGER LOGIC
I recommend your developer use three separate concepts:
1. Trip calculation
What is actually owed from this particular trip?
Example:
Vehicle Hire ₹10,000 − Commission ₹500 = ₹9,500
2. Previous ledger balance
What was already outstanding before this transaction?
Example:
₹1,500 Payable
3. Actual transaction/payment
What did you actually pay or receive today?
Example:
Paid ₹2,000
These should never be mixed together.
Example
Trip:
Net Payable = ₹9,500
Previous:
Payable = ₹1,500
Therefore:
Total Outstanding = ₹11,000 Payable
Today you pay:
₹2,000	

This gives you the flexibility you wanted: you can pay ₹500, ₹2,000, ₹10,000 or the entire amount whenever you actually make the payment.
